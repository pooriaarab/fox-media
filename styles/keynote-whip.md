# Keynote whip

Reference builds: `foxpilot-media/social2/foxpilot/build.py` (v1) and `fox-media/work/foxmate/proj/build2.py` (polish round).

- **Ground:** light grey stage `#f2f4f7`, white cards with a soft drop shadow (`feDropShadow` dy 24, blur 28, 16 % ink). One accent, `#d9480f`.
- **Type pair:** Montserrat 800 for display, JetBrains Mono for labels and the end card.
- **Entrances:** masked per-word rise, a velocity-matched waterfall, blur-to-sharp slams, a cursor that moves and clicks, a prompt that slides up, a lock that slams, chain lines that draw on, a ring with a ticking counter.
- **Seams:** expo-in whips with blur. The direction alternates (left, up, right, down).
- **Presenter:** a 330 px rounded picture-in-picture at the bottom right with a name tag. Music ducks to 0.26 under the voice. SFX stay at 0.3 or less, and at 0.22 or less under the voice.
- **Labels:** small caps mono kicker at the top left. Any one shot shows a label or a sentence, never both.
- **Music:** a sparse keynote bed, 105 to 120 BPM, strong first downbeat.
