# Patch bay

First used on foxplug (`work/foxplug/build.py`, 27.0 s). The whole video is a modular-synth patch panel: matte black, metal jacks with small lamps, and thick patch cables that draw on.

**Why this style for this product.** foxplug plugs an agent into web apps. A patch cable is the plainest picture of "a direct connection with nothing in between", and a jack that only takes one cable shows a key pinned to one host. No earlier fox video uses a hardware-panel look.

- **Ground:** one solid matte black `#16171b`. Nothing moves behind the graphics.
- **Palette:** cream text `#f2ead8`, dim `#9a958a`, fox orange `#ff7a1a` (the main cable, the name, Approve), gold `#ffc23d` (keys), teal `#34d1bf` (third cable), lamp green `#3ddc84`, lamp red `#ff5a4f`, steel `#3a3d45` (the server box), card `#2a2c33`.
- **Type pair:** Barlow Condensed 700/600 (display, panel-label feel) and Barlow 600 (labels); JetBrains Mono 500 for the install line, the URL and host tags.
- **The agent:** an orange glowing orb with a cream sparkle. Never a robot.
- **Signature graphics:** patch cables (cubic sag, 16 px, drop shadow) that draw on, with light dots running along them; metal jacks (radial steel ring, black hole) with a lamp that turns green or red; brand marks (Simple Icons, CC0, own colour) on white chips; a steel "server" box that falls off the panel; synth knobs that turn on in turn.
- **Text entrances (none repeats back to back):** finished at frame 0 (poster), mask wipe, stamp, blur-to-sharp name slam with a slide-in line, rise, stepped type wipe, wipe, rise, per-letter rise on the end card.
- **Seams:** hard cuts on the beat grid (112 BPM, 0.5357 s, phase 0.032 s).
- **Pacing:** 9 shots in 27 s. Sentence shots (1, 4, 9) hold 3.75 s or more. Graphic shots with a 2 to 5 word label hold 2.1 to 3.2 s.
- **Copy frame:** PAS. Problem: a server in between. Agitate to solve: the box drops, "no server". Then the name and "Your keys never leave the browser."
- **Music prompt (elevenlabs/music, 27 s, force_instrumental):** "Modular analog synth groove instrumental, 112 BPM, bubbling sequenced synth arpeggio, warm round bass, punchy tight drum machine with crisp claps, bright and curious, confident tech-launch energy. Drums and arpeggio from the very first second, no intro. A lift with fuller drums at 12 seconds. Clean ending on the final bar at 27 seconds. No vocals." Chosen over minimax/music-02 (same prompt) because it lands on a clean final bar at 27 s; the minimax take runs to its end at full level.
- **SFX cue map:** whoosh as the key slides into the server; pop and stamp on "no server"; hit on the name; click when the key plugs in, glitch when it bounces off; soft pops on the 8 chips; clicks on the 3 knobs; click on Approve, chime on the green lamp; sparkle on the end card. 0.2 to 0.45.
