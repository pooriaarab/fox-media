# agentic-address — 50s postal musical / sorter hall

World 11/20. No VO. 10 shots, ~22 s. Hard cuts.
Shares a decade with speech, never a world: sun
hall vs night studio, song vs drama.

Premise (true, repo desc): verify post addresses
with Lob or PostGrid before you mail. Vendors as
route words only.

## Style anchor

AN: "sunny 50s kodachrome post hall, sorter
clatter, canvas belts race, visored clerk,
pigeonholes to ceiling, dust motes in sun"
NEG: N0 + no digital slick, no night noir.
Hero: same clerk, 3/4, visor + bow tie, age 40s.
One ref.
Prop: smudged parcel. Illegible in shot 2,
pinned exact on map in shot 5.
