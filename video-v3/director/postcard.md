# agentic-postcard — 60s roadside romance / highway

World 17/20. No VO. 10 shots, ~22 s. Hard cuts.
Third 60s world: open highway vs office and
dossier room. Sun and chrome, not keys and oak.

Premise: mail real cards that land like gifts.
Proof is [MEASURED] cards sent on fixture.

## Style anchor

AN: "bright 60s kodachrome roadside, motel neon,
chrome convertible, scarfed driver, signs blur
past, giant postcard flips, dusk glows gold"
NEG: N0 + no urban grit, no night noir.
Hero: same driver, 3/4, scarf + shades, age 30s.
One ref.
Prop: giant card. Blank back in shot 1, stamped
and postmarked in shot 5.

## First frame = poster

Graphic: giant blank card flips over highway.
Hook (6w): "Email deletes itself. Mail stays."
