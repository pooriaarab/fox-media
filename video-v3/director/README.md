before you build that video. Do not mix looks.

## Global rules (all 20)

- No VO. Music, SFX, kinetic text, visuals only.
  Exception: the 4 HeyGen packs carry founder cutaways
  that add new story, never read screen text.
- 20 to 24 s, 10 shots, hard cuts. Core: 12 s, 6 shots.
- Shot 1 is the hook. No fade from black. Frame 0 is
  the poster: finished graphic plus hook line. Export
  `<pack>-poster.png` next to the mp4.
- Text: max 8 words a frame, hold 2.5 s plus 0.3 s
  per word past 6. Max 40% of shots carry a line.
- One visual action per shot. No stacked layers.
- Real logos composited from SVG. Never generate a
  logo or exact text. Typeset wordmarks where no SVG
  exists. 0-1 real screenshots per video, as proof.
- Hero anchor star: one reference face per video,
  every shot edits that ref. No frame chaining.
- 16x9 and 9x16 render apart. No blur fill, no crop.
- Proof numbers are [MEASURED] from repo tests or
  artifacts only. Never invent a count or a claim.
- Every claim about a real product or event is true
  (trends file, Oct 10 2026). Never name a firm as
  breached unless it is public record.
- End card: `npm i agentic-<pack>` plus
  `github.com/pooriaarab/agentic-<pack>`, held 4 s
  minimum. No plea, no tagline, no thanks. Stop.
- Audio: voice over music at 0.26 duck, SFX at or
  under 0.35 under voice, -16 to -14 LUFS, peak
  at or under -1 dB. Audit every render.
